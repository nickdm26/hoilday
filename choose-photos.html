Choose photos

Swap any photo you don't like. Your choices stay put, even when the picker runs again.

  1. Pick a place.
  2. Tap ✕ on a photo you don't like. It's never picked again, and the next best one takes its place.
  3. Or tap Show other photos and tap the one you want.
  4. Tap Save photos.js and save it over js/photos.js. Reload the trip site to see it.
  5. Commit js/photos.js so the change is kept.
