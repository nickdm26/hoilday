## 1. Top activities per place, with prices and booking tips

### Takeaway
The strongest fits for Nick are Gardaland (Lake Garda), the Motor Valley car museums (from Bologna or Modena), Rome (Vatican, Borghese, Colosseum, plus food and nightlife), Florence (Uffizi plus food), and Naples (pizza, Pompeii). Every big museum needs timed advance booking. Borghese has no walk-ins at all.

### Cited Findings
**Rome**
- Vatican Museums: €20 plus a €5 online pre-sale fee (€25). Slots release 60 days ahead, and spring morning slots sell out weeks early — [ArtVisitGuide Rome 2026](https://www.artvisitguide.com/rome-museum-tickets-2026/). Conflicting source: €17 plus a €4 fee, and an early-access 7:30 slot for €38 — [ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html). Check the official tickets.museivaticani.va. The official site failed to load in this session.
- Vatican free day is the last Sunday of every month, 9:00-14:00 (last entry 12:30). Queues often run over 2 hours, and Easter Sunday and some religious holidays are excluded — [ArtVisitGuide](https://www.artvisitguide.com/rome-museum-tickets-2026/).
- Colosseum, Forum and Palatine: €18 plus a €2 online fee (€20). Booking opens 30 days ahead, entry is a 30-minute timed window, the ticket is valid 24h for Forum and Palatine, and it is non-refundable. The first Sunday of the month is free but has long queues — [ArtVisitGuide](https://www.artvisitguide.com/rome-museum-tickets-2026/).
- Galleria Borghese (Bernini, Caravaggio): €16 plus a €2 mandatory booking fee (€18). There are five 2-hour sessions daily at 09:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00 and 17:00, capped at 360 people. Booking runs on a rolling window of about 30 days, there are **no walk-ins**, and peak slots go within hours — [ArtVisitGuide](https://www.artvisitguide.com/rome-museum-tickets-2026/). ItalyPlanner says slots sell out 2-4 weeks ahead in season — [ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html).
- First-Sunday free entry applies to all state museums and archaeological parks. The Vatican is separate, and its free day is the last Sunday — [ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html).

**Florence**
- Uffizi: €25 (Mar-Oct) or €12 (Nov-Feb), plus a €4 booking fee. EU 18-25s pay €2, which doesn't apply to Nick. Book at uffizi.it at least 48h ahead in season — [ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html).
- Accademia (David): €16 peak or €8 off-peak, plus a €4 fee — [ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html).

**Naples, Pompeii and Amalfi**
- Pompeii: €18 plus a €2 fee, booked at pompeiisites.org. The Pompeii + Herculaneum combo is €22 — [ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html).
- The Naples-Pompeii-Sorrento Circumvesuviana is run by EAV (eavsrl.it). It is a regional fixed fare, amount not listed — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Italy-trains.htm).

**Motor Valley (Maranello, Modena, Sant'Agata)**
- Museo Ferrari Maranello entry plus shuttle from Modena station: about US$43.69. Maranello + Museo Enzo Ferrari (Modena) combo: from about US$60.50. Enzo Ferrari Museum alone: from about US$30.25. Enzo + Maranello + F1 simulator: from about US$89 — [Tiqets](https://www.tiqets.com/en/museo-ferrari-maranello-entry-ticket-shuttle-modena-station-ferrari-b18329/).
- Lamborghini Museum + Ferrari Museum + F1 simulator: from about US$163. Lamborghini entry + driving simulation + Ferrari Museum: from about US$73. Ferrari Portofino test drive + museum + simulator: from about US$193 — [Tiqets](https://www.tiqets.com/en/museo-ferrari-maranello-entry-ticket-shuttle-modena-station-ferrari-b18329/).
- Museo Ferrari is open 10:00-18:00 daily. Maranello driving experiences run 09:30-19:00 — [Tiqets](https://www.tiqets.com/en/museo-ferrari-maranello-entry-ticket-shuttle-modena-station-ferrari-b18329/).
- The shuttle leaves from Modena station (pensilina/gate 4). Listed Modena departures were only 10:40 and 15:20. A 2024 reviewer reported 3 shuttles a day and waits of over an hour. Public bus alternatives are lines 410, 13, 7 and 760 from Modena, and line 800 from Maranello station — [Tiqets](https://www.tiqets.com/en/museo-ferrari-maranello-entry-ticket-shuttle-modena-station-ferrari-b18329/).
- Bologna-based day tours of the Ferrari, Lamborghini and Pagani museums are sold on Expedia — [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com.my/ms/things-to-do/ferrari-and-lamborghini-museums-pagani-factory-museum-tour-from-bologna.a3095304.activity-details).

### Inferences
- **Rome fit: very high (3 nights).** Book Borghese and the Vatican first, because they are the hardest. The 15:00 or 17:00 Borghese slot suits a non-morning person. A Vatican afternoon slot avoids the 7:30 early-access option.
- **Vatican on 25 Apr 2027:** this is both Liberation Day and the last Sunday of April. It may be a free-entry day, but it would be very crowded, and holiday exclusions could apply. Avoid it, or check the closure calendar.
- **Motor Valley fit: very high, for a car fan.** Without a car, the Modena shuttle timetable is the bottleneck. A Bologna-departing tour that bundles Ferrari, Lamborghini and Pagani is the low-effort option. It costs more but saves a tiring logistics day. Sant'Agata (Lamborghini) has poor public transport, which is background knowledge and unverified.
- **Florence fit: high (2 nights).** Uffizi in April is at the €25 high-season price. Book it 1-2 weeks ahead.
- **Naples, Pompeii and Amalfi fit: medium-high.** Naples is the pizza mecca. Pompeii is flat but has a lot of walking on uneven stones, so take it easy on the back and do half a day. The Amalfi Coast in late April is pretty but involves stairs and buses. Treat it as a scenic day trip or ferry rather than a base, given the budget.
- **Milan fit: medium.** Its main value is as the rail hub for Gardaland (about 50-60 min) and for nightlife (Navigli aperitivo). The Last Supper needs booking well ahead. Price not verified this session.
- **Venice extras fit: medium.** The Contiki covers the basics. If he extends a night, the extras are Generator Venice (Giudecca) and cicchetti bar-hopping. Spritz and cicchetti crawls are background knowledge, unverified.

## 2. Gardaland: 2027 opening, coasters, train access

### Takeaway
Gardaland opened its 2026 season on 28 Mar 2026, so 2027 will very likely open around Easter (27-28 Mar 2027). The 2027 date is not yet announced. Nick's dates (23-28 Apr) are safely inside the season. Getting there is easy: a train to Peschiera del Garda, then a free shuttle.

### Cited Findings
- The 2026 summer season was planned to start on **28 March 2026** (article published 28 Jan 2026). It contains no 2027 information — [EAP Magazin](https://www.eap-magazin.de/Article/Gardaland-Reports-Positive-Results-for-%E2%80%9CMagic-Winter%E2%80%9D.html).
- Hours (2026), late March to mid-June: daily 10:30-18:00. Ticket offices close 2h before park closing — [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/milan/gardaland-park/).
- Gate price (2026) is €56 for adults. Online prices are lower and fall the earlier you book. Parking is about €7 — [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/milan/gardaland-park/).
- Top coasters — [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/milan/gardaland-park/):
  - **Raptor:** wing coaster, 140-195 cm.
  - **Oblivion:** vertical dive coaster, 140-195 cm.
  - **Blue Tornado:** suspended looping coaster, 140-200 cm.
  - **Shaman:** steel coaster.
  - **Mammut:** mine train, 1,000 m or more.
  - **Wolf Legend:** newer addition.
- Train: Milan-Venice line to **Peschiera del Garda** station, about 2 km away. A **free shuttle bus** runs about every 30 min, starting 30 min before opening, with the last return 30 min after closing. From Milano Centrale it takes about 50-60 min — [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/milan/gardaland-park/).
- Tips: mid-April to late May weekdays are quieter, so do Raptor, Oblivion and Blue Tornado first. Use the official app for queue times. Entry is free on your birthday with ID — [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/milan/gardaland-park/).

### Inferences
- **Fit: top pick.** Go on a weekday, not 25 Apr (a Sunday and a holiday) or 1 May. The 10:30 opening suits a non-morning person.
- Base options: Peschiera or Verona (one stop away, cheap hostels), or a day trip from Milan or Venice. Sirmione is next to Peschiera, so a spa rest day pairs well with Gardaland.
- These are Italy's biggest coasters, but by European standards they are mid-sized, with no giga or hyper coasters. Background-knowledge stats, unverified: Raptor about 33 m, Oblivion about 42 m drop.

### Gaps
- The 2027 opening date and 2027 prices are unannounced as of Oct 2026. Watch gardaland.it from about January 2027.
- Official coaster speed and height stats were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Example cheapest advance fares (no refunds or changes) — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Italy-trains.htm):
  - Rome-Florence: €19.90
  - Florence-Venice: €19.90
  - Rome-Venice: €29.90
  - Naples-Florence: €29.90
- Booking opens up to 4 months ahead. This shrinks to about 30 days right after the June and December timetable changes — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Italy-trains.htm).
- Use Trainline or Rail Europe to compare Italo and Trenitalia. Trenitalia's site shows only its own trains, and Italo isn't covered by Interrail — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Italy-trains.htm).
- Since Aug 2023, regional tickets bought online are tied to the train chosen. Paper tickets bought at the station must be validated before boarding — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Italy-trains.htm).

## 6. Events in Italy in April 2027

### Takeaway
The main April 2027 events are Easter (28 Mar), Rome's birthday (21 Apr) and Liberation Day (25 Apr). Rome's birthday programme is free. Its big parade usually runs on the Sunday before 21 April, which would be 18 Apr 2027, when Nick is on Contiki. Weekday re-enactments could still be on 21 Apr.

### Cited Findings
- The 2026 Natale di Roma ran 18-21 Apr 2026, with the parade on Sunday 19 Apr and the main events on 21 Apr. Locations included the Circus Maximus, Imperial Fora and Pantheon. It featured the "largest historical parade in Europe", a gladiator tournament and re-enactments by more than 1,300 re-enactors from 16 countries. **All events were free.** The programme is posted at natalidiroma.it — [Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/events/birthday-rome-26th-edition-historical-re-enactment-gruppo-storico-romano).
- First-Sunday free state museums: 4 Apr and 2 May 2027 would apply, following the 2026 pattern — [ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html).

### Inferences
- **25 Apr 2027 (Sunday, Liberation Day):** a public holiday with marches, concerts and crowds. Some shops close, and trains and parks are busier. Avoid Gardaland and travelling that day.
- **Easter 2027 (28 Mar)** falls before his Italy dates, so it matters only for the snowboarding leg. Gardaland likely opens that weekend.
- 1 May 2027 (Saturday) is a public holiday. Rome usually holds a big free Concertone at San Giovanni (background knowledge, unverified). It is relevant only if he extends.

### Gaps
- The 2027 Natale di Roma dates are not published.
- No verified list of 2027 April festivals elsewhere, for example Vinitaly in Verona (usually April) or Milan Design Week (usually April). Background knowledge says Milan Design Week (Salone del Mobile) typically falls in mid-April and makes Milan hostels very expensive. Check the 2027 dates.
