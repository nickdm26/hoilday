# France and Switzerland picks for Nick (solo, 31, NZ) - April 2027

Context: Contiki 11-20 Apr 2027 includes 2 nights Lucerne + 2 nights Paris. All dates below are 2026 season dates and are used to infer April 2027 (flagged where inferred). Prices are 2026. Research date: Oct 2026.

## 1. Swiss mountain excursions in April: what's open, prices, pass value

### Takeaway
Pilatus in mid-April is the aerial cableway from Kriens only. The cogwheel railway (and so the classic Golden Round Trip) usually opens in mid-May, and the 2026 season started 11 May. Jungfraujoch is open all year, but it is very expensive at ~CHF 224 return from Interlaken in low season (Jan-Apr). A Half Fare Card halves it, and is the best-value pass for a short, budget Swiss stint built around one or two mountain days.

### Cited Findings
- Pilatus cogwheel railway (Alpnachstad) operated **11 May - 29 Nov 2026**, so it will almost certainly be closed in mid-April 2027 (inferred) — [MySwissAlps Pilatus](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/pilatus/)
- Pilatus aerial cableway from Kriens runs most of the year. 2026 maintenance breaks were 30-31 Mar and 19 Oct-27 Nov. The same page also says "except for a few days in late April, you can reach the summit year-round", which contradicts the listed breaks, so check pilatus.ch for April 2027 — [MySwissAlps Pilatus](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/pilatus/)
- Pilatus return ticket Dec 2025-Apr 2026 (cable car only): **CHF 60 full, CHF 30 with Half Fare or Swiss Travel Pass**. May-Nov: CHF 84 / CHF 42 — [MySwissAlps Pilatus](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/pilatus/)
- Golden Round Trip 2026 (boat + cogwheel + cableway, Lucerne-Lucerne, 2nd class; runs only in the cogwheel season): CHF 119.80 full / 59.90 Half Fare / 42 Swiss Travel Pass. Silver Round Trip: CHF 94.20 / 47.10 / 42 — [MySwissAlps Pilatus](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/pilatus/)
- Pass discounts on Pilatus: Swiss Travel Pass gives 50% off the mountain legs and makes the Lucerne-Kriens bus and the Lucerne-Alpnachstad train/boat free. Half Fare gives 50% off everything — [MySwissAlps Pilatus](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/pilatus/)
- Jungfraujoch return 2026, Jan-Apr / May-Oct: Interlaken Ost **CHF 224.40 / 261.20 full**. From Wengen/Grindelwald: CHF 201.20 / 239.20 full; with Swiss Travel Pass CHF 148.60 / 177.20 (25% off); **with Half Fare CHF 100.60 / 119.60** (50% off the whole trip) — [MySwissAlps Jungfraujoch](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/jungfraujoch/)
- Jungfraujoch seat reservation is CHF 10, mandatory 1 May-31 Oct and optional in April. The Good Morning ticket (~20% off) is not offered Jan-Apr — [MySwissAlps Jungfraujoch](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/jungfraujoch/)
- Fastest route: Interlaken Ost - Grindelwald Terminal - Eiger Express cable car - Eigergletscher - cogwheel train to the top, ~1h37 one way. Via Lauterbrunnen/Wengen or Grindelwald/Kleine Scheidegg it is ~2h07. All routes cost the same, so go up one way and down the other. Jungfraujoch is open year-round (Ice Palace, platforms). In 2026 the Grindelwald-Kleine Scheidegg cogwheel closed **27 Apr-8 May**, which may affect late-April 2027 plans (inferred) — [MySwissAlps Jungfraujoch](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/jungfraujoch/)
- Swiss Travel Pass 2026, adult 2nd class continuous: 3 days CHF 254, 4 days CHF 309, 6 days CHF 395, 8 days CHF 437. Flex costs slightly more. The pass gives "up to 50%" off mountain railways, and panorama trains need paid seat reservations — [Trvlogue STP 2026](https://www.trvlogue.com/en/swiss-travel-pass-2026-guide)

### Inferences
- **Pilatus in April verdict:** still worth it as an easy, no-hike summit: bus from Lucerne to Kriens, then gondola and the "Dragon Ride" aerial cableway, about CHF 30 with Half Fare. But it is not the famous cogwheel/boat Golden Round Trip, so manage expectations. If the Contiki includes a Pilatus or Titlis excursion, skip doing it again yourself.
- **Jungfraujoch verdict:** the iconic "Top of Europe" ride is ideal for someone who wants no hiking, but it costs ~CHF 112 with Half Fare from Interlaken (half of 224.40) versus ~CHF 224 without. Only do it on a clear-weather day, and check the webcam that morning. Leave mid-morning to suit someone who isn't a morning person: the Eiger Express route takes ~1h37.
- **Pass choice:** a short Swiss leg (Lucerne via Contiki plus 2-3 nights Interlaken plus maybe Zurich) favours the Half Fare Card. It halves the Jungfraujoch (saving ~CHF 112) plus the trains, versus a CHF 254+ Swiss Travel Pass that only gives 25% off Jungfraujoch. Check whether Contiki already covers Swiss transport between stops.
- Rigi (cogwheel from Vitznau/Arth-Goldau) is commonly cited as year-round and free with the Swiss Travel Pass (background knowledge, unverified here). It would be the April alternative to the Pilatus cogwheel.

- Not verified: Rigi and Schilthorn (Piz Gloria, ~CHF 100+ from Lauterbrunnen) April operation and 2026 prices. The Schilthorn cableway usually has a spring maintenance closure in April or May, so check schilthorn.ch for 2027.

## 2. Interlaken / Lauterbrunnen: paragliding, valley, hostels

### Takeaway
Tandem paragliding in Interlaken costs ~CHF 160-220 and runs in spring, but April weather is variable. Operators warn people with significant back problems against it, which matters for Nick. Balmers is the standout social hostel, rated 9.6 from 8,000+ reviews.

### Cited Findings
- Tandem paragliding prices: standard 10-15 min **CHF 160-180**, scenic 20-25 min CHF 190-220, thermal 30-45 min CHF 240-280. Photos/video cost CHF 30-50 extra, and the whole outing takes 2-3 hours door to door — [tour-switzerland.ch](https://www.tour-switzerland.ch/guides/paragliding-interlaken/)
- Spring (April-May): thermals are building, the weather is more variable, and morning flights are more reliable. Operations "reduce significantly" Nov-Mar, which implies flights run in April, weather permitting — [tour-switzerland.ch](https://www.tour-switzerland.ch/guides/paragliding-interlaken/)
- **Not recommended for people with "significant back or neck problems."** Passenger weight limit is usually 40 to 100-110 kg. Operators include Paragliding Jungfrau (launching from Beatenberg) and Skywings — [tour-switzerland.ch](https://www.tour-switzerland.ch/guides/paragliding-interlaken/)
- Balmers Hostel: rated **9.6 "Superb" from 8,167 reviews** (atmosphere 9.7). It has a small bar and an on-site guest-only club that opens 1-2x/week depending on season, plus a summer beer garden, a big kitchen, a terrace and social activities. Guests must be 18+. Some reviewers say rooms get hot (no AC). Check-in 16:00-00:00, check-out by 10:00 — [Hostelworld Balmers](https://www.hostelworld.com/hosteldetails.php/Balmers-Hostel/Interlaken/715)

### Inferences
- Paragliding is a "maybe": have Nick ask the operator about his back. The landing is in central Interlaken (Höhematte), but launches and landings can jolt.
- Lauterbrunnen valley: the flat valley-floor walk to Staubbach Falls is short and easy, and Trümmelbach Falls (inside the mountain, by lift) usually opens in early to mid-April. Neither is verified here.
- Interlaken (2-3 nights at Balmers) works as both a social base and a mountain base. One Jungfraujoch day plus one rest/lake day fits his profile.

### Gaps
- Balmers dorm price not shown. Expect roughly CHF 40-60/night (unverified).
- Trümmelbach Falls and Lauterbrunnen April opening not verified.

## 3. Zurich: Lindt Home of Chocolate, Bernina Express, Zermatt

### Takeaway
The Lindt Home of Chocolate (CHF 17, daily 10:00-19:00, Kilchberg) is a must for a sweet tooth and needs a timed booking. The Bernina Express and Zermatt are scenic splurges that fit poorly with a short, budget Swiss stay.

### Cited Findings
- Lindt Home of Chocolate: **adult CHF 17**, open daily **10:00-19:00**. Ticket covers 7 "chocolate worlds", tasting and a view of the production line. The 9 m chocolate fountain, shop and café are free without a ticket. Chocolateria courses are booked separately. It's in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich, ~17 min from Zurich HB. Demand is "very high", so book a time slot ahead. Zürich Card gives 20% off — [Zürich Tourism](https://www.zuerich.com/en/visit/culture/lindt-home-of-chocolate)
- Bernina Express runs Chur-Tirano (Milan connection) and needs a seat reservation (fee not stated) — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Switzerland)

### Inferences
- **Lindt: strong yes.** It has the latest opening hours of any pick, which suits a non-morning person.
- **Bernina Express:** spectacular but a full day of sitting (~4h Chur-Tirano). It's worth it only if routing to or from Milan anyway. Verdict: optional, probably skip for budget.
- **Zermatt/Matterhorn:** far from the Lucerne/Interlaken axis and very expensive (Matterhorn Glacier Paradise ~CHF 100+, unverified). It's redundant if he does Jungfraujoch. Verdict: skip.

## 4. Paris: Louvre, Orsay, Disneyland, Parc Astérix, pastries, nightlife, hostels

### Takeaway
The Louvre costs €32 for non-EU visitors from 14 Jan 2026, and timed booking is effectively required. Disneyland's second park reopened as Disney Adventure World on 29 Mar 2026 with World of Frozen, which is not a coaster. The big coasters are at Parc Astérix: Toutatis (107 km/h, France's fastest), OzIris and Tonnerre de Zeus. Astérix is open from early April, on select days in spring.

### Cited Findings
- Louvre: from **14 Jan 2026**, non-EU visitors pay **€32** (up 45% from €22). Guided groups pay €28 and are capped at 20. The money funds the "Louvre - New Renaissance" modernisation plan, and the article mentions no closures — [ABC/AP](https://abc13.com/post/louvre-museum-paris-hikes-ticket-prices-most-european-tourists-what-know/18401055/)
- Disneyland Paris: Walt Disney Studios was renamed **Disney Adventure World on 29 Mar 2026**. New: World of Frozen (Frozen Ever After boat ride), Raiponce Tangled Spin, Regal View restaurant and lounge (the first bar inside a park), and the Disney Cascade of Lights night show with drones, fountains and pyro. Avengers Campus includes the reimagined Rock 'n' Roller Coaster (Iron Man/Avengers). An "Up" carousel is likely in 2027. Lion King Pride Lands is expected 2028 or later — [Disney Tourist Blog](https://www.disneytouristblog.com/new-rides-lands-future-disneyland-paris); [Journal du Geek](https://www.journaldugeek.com/2026/05/13/disneyland-paris-asterix-europa-park-et-parc-spirou-tour-dhorizon-des-nouveautes-2026/)
- Parc Astérix coasters: **Toutatis** (multi-launch, 51 m, 107 km/h, France's fastest), **OzIris** (inverted, 40 m, 90 km/h), **Tonnerre de Zeus** (wooden, described as Europe's 2nd-longest), Goudurix (looping) — [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/paris/parc-asterix/)
- Parc Astérix 2026 season: opened **4 Apr 2026**, with April-May hours **10:00-18:00 on select days**. Tickets: **Futé €56** (book 7+ days ahead, fixed date), Liberté Flex €68. Getting there: RER B to CDG Terminal 1, then the park shuttle — [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/paris/parc-asterix/)

Planned: the Greece zone overhaul in **2027**, and a Londinium indoor zone with an indoor launch coaster in **2028** — [Journal du Geek](https://www.journaldugeek.com/2026/05/13/disneyland-paris-asterix-europa-park-et-parc-spirou-tour-dhorizon-des-nouveautes-2026/)

## 5. Cité de l'Automobile (Mulhouse), train links, other places he may have missed

### Takeaway
The Cité de l'Automobile is the world's largest car museum (500+ cars, ~150 Bugattis, €19) and sits right on the Paris-Basel TGV Lyria line, so it's an easy stopover between Paris and Switzerland. Paris-Basel takes ~3h, from €29.

### Cited Findings
- Cité de l'Automobile (Schlumpf collection), Mulhouse: **500+ vehicles, ~150 Bugattis including two Bugatti Royales**. Adult €19, open daily (hours vary by season; closed 25 Dec). Address: 17 rue de la Mertzau — [JDS](https://www.jds.fr/mulhouse/musee/cite-de-l-automobile-musee-de-l-auto-37_L)
- TGV Lyria Paris-Basel ~3h04 and Paris-Zurich ~4h04, **from €29** in 2nd class. Trains call at **Mulhouse**. Basel-Lucerne is hourly, ~1h02, ~€29.60 full fare — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Switzerland)

### Inferences
- Stopover idea: Paris → Mulhouse (~2h40 by TGV, inferred from the Paris-Basel time) → Cité de l'Automobile (3-4 h; also the Cité du Train railway museum) → Basel (~20 min) → Lucerne/Interlaken. Store luggage at Mulhouse station.
- Basel: big art (Kunstmuseum, Fondation Beyeler). Unverified, but a good art/budget alternative to Zurich.
- Missed options for his profile:
  - **Nice/French Riviera**: beaches, nightlife, social hostels. The sea is cold in April (~15°C, unverified), and the Riviera has no real surf; Biarritz/Hossegor on the Atlantic is the French surf hub, but it is cold and wetsuit-only in April.
  - **Lyon**: France's food capital, with bouchons and Les Halles Paul Bocuse; 2h TGV from Paris.
  - **Europa-Park** (Rust, Germany, ~1h from Basel/Mulhouse): Europe's best theme park for coasters (Silver Star; Monaco land new in 2026 per [Journal du Geek](https://www.journaldugeek.com/2026/05/13/disneyland-paris-asterix-europa-park-et-parc-spirou-tour-dhorizon-des-nouveautes-2026/)). It fits his coaster love perfectly and pairs well with Mulhouse and Basel. Strongly flag it.
- Thermal spa options are unverified. In Switzerland, Bad Ragaz Tamina Therme, Leukerbad, or Zurich's Thermalbad & Spa Zürich (Hürlimann, rooftop pool). In Paris, the Hammam Pacha or Les Bains.
