## King's Day 2027 (Koningsdag) and King's Night: what happens, where to be, prices, solo tips

### Takeaway
King's Day 2027 is **Tuesday 27 April 2027**, and King's Night (Koningsnacht) is the evening of **Monday 26 April**. Amsterdam becomes the world's biggest street party: over a million people, orange everywhere, a citywide free market, canal-boat parties and stages. It's a strong fit for Nick: nightlife, social, no early start needed. His main risk is accommodation, which books out months ahead at much higher prices. He should book a bed for 26–27 Apr (ideally 25–28 Apr) as soon as possible.

### Cited Findings
- Calendar check: 27 Apr 2027 = Tuesday, 26 Apr 2027 = Monday, 12 Apr 2027 = Monday (computed with Python's datetime). King's Day moves to 26 Apr only when 27 Apr falls on a Sunday, which doesn't apply in 2027. — [LuggageHero guide](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/)
- The holiday honours King Willem-Alexander's birthday. King's Night parties start the evening before, and the main day runs from early morning to late evening. — [LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/)
- The guide calls it "the world's largest open-air festival" with over 1 million visitors. Orange clothing ("oranjegekte") is standard and sold everywhere on the day. — [LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/)
- Vrijmarkt (free market): anyone can sell on the street without a permit that day, from second-hand goods to homemade food. — [LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/)
- Where to be, per [LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/):
  - **Jordaan:** canal-side parties with a local feel.
  - **Canals:** hundreds of boats. Book a party boat early, since last-minute tickets cost a premium.
  - **Rembrandtplein and Leidseplein:** outdoor stages, food stalls and bars spilling into the streets.
  - **Museumplein:** organised festivals and concerts with the biggest crowds.
  - **Waterlooplein:** a younger crowd.
  - **Vondelpark:** family and kids' market, so it's the least relevant for Nick.
- Accommodation: hotels and hostels fill months ahead and prices rise significantly. The source gives no figures. — [LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/)
- Transport: many tram and bus routes change, so the metro is usually best. — [LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/)
- Tips, all from [LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/):
  - Arrive for King's Night or stay a day after.
  - Bring cash, because vendors are often cash-only and ATM queues are long.
  - Bring layers and a rain jacket.
  - Watch for pickpockets.
  - Set meeting points, because mobile networks get congested.
  - Expect toilet queues; some bars allow use only with a purchase.
  - Bags may be searched and glass is banned.
  - Hotel luggage storage fills up.

### Inferences
- For a solo traveller the social route is a party hostel with a bar or pub crawl, such as St Christopher's at The Winston or Flying Pig (see the hostels section). He'd then have a ready-made group for King's Night and the day. King's Night itself is the more club and bar-centred night, which suits a non-morning person.
- Dorm prices in Amsterdam on King's Day are commonly reported at 2–3x normal rates, and some places set minimum stays (background knowledge, unverified; no source gave figures). Budget workaround: stay in Haarlem, Utrecht or Leiden and train in. Trains get very crowded, so allow extra time.
- Pacing: plan a rest day on 28 Apr.
- Background knowledge, unverified: Amsterdam usually holds big organised King's Day dance festivals (ticketed), and many clubs run King's Night specials. Check 2027 line-ups around Feb–Mar 2027.

## Efteling and Walibi Holland: coasters, April opening, train access from Amsterdam

### Takeaway
**Efteling** (Kaatsheuvel) is the Netherlands' top theme park and is open in April. Its big coasters are Baron 1898 (dive), Python, the Joris en de Draak wooden racer and De Vliegende Hollander, and it's beautifully themed. **Walibi Holland** (Biddinghuizen) is the thrill park: Goliath (billed as the Benelux's highest and fastest), Untamed (RMC hybrid), Lost Gravity and Speed of Sound. Its season starts around late March or early April, often on limited weekend days. For Nick's "big coasters" brief, Walibi has the more intense rides and Efteling the better full day out. If he can do only one, Efteling is the safer pick for April and easier to reach from Amsterdam.

### Cited Findings
- Efteling coasters, per [Theme Park Insider](https://themeparkinsider.com/reviews/efteling):
  - **Baron 1898:** dive coaster, 123 ft drop, 56 mph, 2 inversions.
  - **Python:** Vekoma, 95 ft, 47 mph, 4 inversions.
  - **Joris en de Draak:** wooden racing coaster, 72 ft drops, 47 mph.
  - **Vogel Rok:** indoor coaster, 40 mph.
  - **De Vliegende Hollander:** water coaster with a wet final drop.
  - **Max + Moritz:** family coaster.
- Efteling tips: it's a big park, so 1–2 days. Head to Symbolica early. End with the Aquanura fountain show. Some attractions close Jan–Apr. — [Theme Park Insider](https://themeparkinsider.com/reviews/efteling)
- Efteling access: train to 's-Hertogenbosch (Den Bosch), then bus 300, 301, 136 or 800 to the park, about 75 min south of Amsterdam. — [Theme Park Insider](https://themeparkinsider.com/reviews/efteling)
- Walibi Holland coasters: Goliath ("fastest and highest roller coaster in the Benelux"), Lost Gravity (2016, 32 m, 2 inversions), Speed of Sound, El Condor, Xpress: Platform 13, Robin Hood. Robin Hood has since been rebuilt as Untamed (see Inferences). — [ThingsToDoInAmsterdam](https://thingstodoinamsterdam.com/?p=315). Untamed and YoY are listed among the current attractions. — [Freizeitpark-Welt](https://www.freizeitpark-welt.de/freizeitparks/walibi_holland/preise/)
- Walibi 2026 prices: adult gate €46. Dated online tickets start at €44.50, SocialDeal €34.50, and promos go as low as €32.50. — [Freizeitpark-Welt](https://www.freizeitpark-welt.de/freizeitparks/walibi_holland/preise/); [MyDealz/SocialDeal](https://www.mydealz.de/deals/socialdeal-eintrittsticket-walibi-holland-fur-3450-eur-statt-46-eur-2819804)
- Walibi access: train from Amsterdam Centraal to Harderwijk, then bus 247 (Walibi Express) to the gate. The source quotes about 2 h total, but the info is dated (2018 season). — [ThingsToDoInAmsterdam](https://thingstodoinamsterdam.com/?p=315)

### Inferences
- Background knowledge, unverified:
  - Untamed is a 2019 RMC hybrid conversion of Robin Hood and is widely rated Walibi's best coaster.
  - Efteling is open daily all year, with April hours roughly 10:00–18:00.
  - Efteling adult tickets are about €45–60 in 2026, dated and cheaper online.
  - Walibi's spring calendar usually has closed weekdays in early April.
  - Walibi buses also run from Lelystad Centrum in some seasons.
- Check efteling.com and walibi.nl calendars once the 2027 calendars are published (usually around Dec–Jan).
- Late opening (10:00) suits a non-morning person, but rope-drop gives the shortest queues. A weekday visit (Tue–Thu, outside Dutch school holidays) is best. Dutch May holiday (meivakantie) typically covers the last week of April into early May, so parks are busier around King's Day.
- Back-issue caveat: intense coasters (Untamed's airtime, Goliath, Python's older Vekoma ride) may jar a bad back. Nick should judge for himself.

## Museum booking needs and prices (Van Gogh, Rijksmuseum) + car museum

### Takeaway
Both Museumplein museums use timed online tickets. The Rijksmuseum is €25 (cashless, 9:00–17:00 daily) and the Van Gogh Museum about €25 (reseller €27). The Van Gogh Museum is the one that sells out; book it 1–2 weeks ahead. The Louwman Museum in The Hague (about 250 classic cars, €20) is a must-add for a car-museum fan.

### Cited Findings
- Rijksmuseum, per [ArtVisitGuide](https://www.artvisitguide.com/rijksmuseum-tickets-guide/):
  - €25 adult. All tickets are timed and sold online.
  - Open daily 09:00–17:00. Cashless.
  - Peak season (Apr–Aug) needs booking 2–3 days ahead.
  - Museumkaart and I Amsterdam City Card holders still need a time slot.
  - GetYourGuide sells it for €27 with free cancellation.
- Van Gogh Museum: timed entry, and slots sell out 7–10 days ahead. The reseller price is €27 with free cancellation. It's a 5-minute walk from the Rijksmuseum. — [ArtVisitGuide](https://www.artvisitguide.com/rijksmuseum-tickets-guide/) (affiliate site). The official Van Gogh page failed to load.
- Louwman Museum (The Hague), per [WhichMuseum](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/louwman-museum-the-hague-130/tickets-prices):
  - Adult €20. Museumkaart valid.
  - "World's oldest private collection of automobiles," about 250 classic cars plus motorcycles and carriages.

### Inferences
- Background knowledge, unverified:
  - The official Van Gogh adult price is about €25.
  - The Van Gogh Museum has Friday late openings, often until 21:00, sometimes with music and bars. That's good for a non-morning person.
  - Louwman is closed Mondays and is about 1 h by train from Amsterdam to Den Haag.
- A Museumkaart (about €75 for Dutch residents; tourists get a temporary card) could pay off if he does Rijks + Van Gogh + Louwman + more (background knowledge, unverified; see [ArtVisitGuide Museumkaart guide](https://www.artvisitguide.com/amsterdam-museumkaart-guide-2026/)).

## Best chocolate, waffles, beer bars and famous food spots

### Takeaway
Brussels is the sweet-tooth hub. Bruges has The Chocolate Line and Choco-Story. Antwerp has Chocolate Nation, billed as the world's largest Belgian chocolate museum. Fries (frites) are the other must. Belgium clearly beats the Netherlands for sweets.

### Cited Findings
- Brussels chocolate, per [Tootbus guide](https://www.tootbus.com/en/brussels/inspiration/mouthwatering-guide-best-chocolate-shops-and-bakeries-brussels):
  - **Mary:** since 1919, pralines and truffles, near Grand Place.
  - **Wittamer:** Sablon, pralines and pastries.
  - **Laurent Gerbaud:** unusual flavours, with hands-on chocolate workshops.
  - **Elisabeth** and **Atelier Sainte Catherine:** affordable artisan shops.
  - **Neuhaus:** iconic Brussels brand.
  - **Choco-Story museum:** includes make-your-own bar or pralines.
- Brussels waffles and biscuits: **Maison Dandoy** (since 1829, waffles and speculoos). Tarte au sucre is a local dessert. — [Tootbus](https://www.tootbus.com/en/brussels/inspiration/mouthwatering-guide-best-chocolate-shops-and-bakeries-brussels)
- Brussels, per [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/what-to-eat-and-drink-in-belgium):
  - **Van Dender:** hot chocolate.
  - **Gaufres & Waffles:** waffles.
  - **Frit Flagey:** classic frituur in Ixelles.
  - **Frites Atelier:** fries.
- Bruges: **The Chocolate Line** (works with the Fat Duck and Noma) and **Choco-Story**. — [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/what-to-eat-and-drink-in-belgium)
- Ghent: **De Frietketel** (frituur) and **Frites Atelier**. — [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/what-to-eat-and-drink-in-belgium)
- Antwerp: **Chocolate Nation** ("world's largest Belgian chocolate museum"), **The Chocolate Line** and **Frites Atelier**. — [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/what-to-eat-and-drink-in-belgium)

### Inferences (background knowledge, unverified; verify before publishing)

## Social hostels and nightlife per city

### Takeaway
Amsterdam has the strongest social hostel scene: St Christopher's at The Winston (nightly pub crawl), Flying Pig Downtown and Uptown, Bulldog and Hans Brinker. Bruges has a surprisingly social pick in St Christopher's Bauhaus, plus Lybeer (pub quizzes, karaoke, beer tastings). Bruges dorms run about €15–23 a night outside events.

### Cited Findings
- Amsterdam party hostels, per [Savvy Backpacker](https://thesavvybackpacker.com/city-guide/best-party-hostels-amsterdam/):
  - **St Christopher's at The Winston:** Warmoesstraat, above Belushi's bar, with a nightly pub crawl, free walking tour and 2-for-1 drinks.
  - **Flying Pig Downtown:** near Centraal, DJ nights, cheap beer.
  - **Flying Pig Uptown:** near Vondelpark and the museums, more local feel.
  - **Bulldog Hostel:** central nightlife, rooftop terrace.
  - **Hans Brinker:** Kerkstraat near Leidseplein, budget.
  - Party hostels fill fast, so book early.
- Bruges, per [Hostelgeeks](https://hostelgeeks.com/best-hostels-in-bruges-belgium/):
  - **Hostel Lybeer:** best for solo travellers; bar, BBQ, beer tastings, karaoke, pub quizzes.
  - **Snuffel:** bar and terrace, breakfast included.
  - **St Christopher's Bauhaus:** the most party-oriented, with a 40+ beer bar and pod beds.
  - Dorms average €15–23 a night and city tax is about €2 per night.

### Inferences (background knowledge, unverified)
- **Brussels:** Meininger, Sleep Well, Jacques Brel and Generator-type hostels. Nightlife is around Saint-Géry/Place Saint-Géry and Delirium Village (Impasse de la Fidélité). Fuse is the famous techno club.
- **Ghent:** Hostel Uppelink (canal views, social), plus the Overpoort student bars. It's lively on a weeknight in term time; Belgian universities are in session in late April.
- **Antwerp:** Antwerp is Belgium's club capital. Kompass Klub is techno; Ampere is under the station. Hostels include Pulcinella and Antwerp Central Youth Hostel.
- **Amsterdam nightlife:** Leidseplein and Rembrandtplein bars; clubs include Paradiso, Melkweg, De School-type venues (De School closed in 2023; check current) and Shelter.

## Fit verdicts and possibly missed places

### Takeaway
- **Must:** Amsterdam for King's Day (26–27 Apr), with a 3-night stay of about 25–28 Apr.
- **Belgium core:** Brussels (2–3 nights), then Bruges or Ghent (2–3 nights). Ghent is the cheaper, livelier student city base, with Bruges as a day trip.

### Inferences (background knowledge, unverified)
- Dutch sauna culture: Dutch saunas are almost always nude and mixed gender, with towels for sitting on. Big day-spa "thermen" complexes include Thermen Bussloo (near Apeldoorn), Elysium (Bleiswijk, near Rotterdam, one of Europe's largest) and Thermae 2000 (Valkenburg). A day pass is about €35–50. Many have a weekly "textile day" when swimwear is required. This is good for Nick's rest days.

- Rotterdam: Markthal, Fenix, Depot Boijmans, Hotel New York. Nightlife: Witte de Withstraat bars, Maassilo/BIRD clubs. It's 40 min from Amsterdam by IC Direct. Combine with The Hague and Louwman.
- Proposed flow (inference only):
  - Contiki Amsterdam about 12 Apr.
  - Belgium leg (Brussels, Ghent/Bruges, Antwerp) mid-April.
  - Efteling, which sits on the Antwerp to Den Bosch side, on the way back north.
  - The Hague or Rotterdam (Louwman + sauna) about 22–25 Apr.
  - Amsterdam 25–28 Apr for King's Day, the museums and Keukenhof (it's open until about 9 May).
