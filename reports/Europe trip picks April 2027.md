### Why these ten win

King's Day tops the list because it ticks nightlife, social and "not a morning person" all at once. It is the **world's largest open-air festival** with over a million people, and the party starts the night before ([LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/)). Rome, Italy is next because it stacks art, food and nightlife in one base. Europa-Park, Germany is the strongest coaster park. Its rides include **Silver Star (a hypercoaster), blue fire, Wodan and Voltron Nevera** ([MonsterDealz](https://www.monsterdealz.de/magazin/europa-park-tickets)). Gardaland, Italy is smaller. Its coasters are mid-sized by European standards (unverified). But it is in Italy and easy to reach.

## Italy: your top priority, with Rome, Bologna and Garda as the core

**Rome, Italy (3 nights).** This is your strongest city. Book the hard tickets first. The **Borghese Gallery has no walk-ins**. It costs €18 (NZ$35) and has five 2-hour slots a day. Peak slots go within hours ([ArtVisitGuide](https://www.artvisitguide.com/rome-museum-tickets-2026/)). Take the 15:00 or 17:00 slot. The Vatican Museums cost about €25 (NZ$49) online. Spring slots sell out weeks ahead. Another source says €21 (NZ$41) ([ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html)). Check the official site. The Colosseum costs €20 (NZ$39). It books 30 days ahead and is non-refundable. For a social bed, The Yellow near Termini has dorms for **about €20-35 (NZ$39-68)** and a rooftop bar ([ItalyPlanner hostels](https://italyplanner.com/best-hostels-italy.html)). Generator Rome and Colors are backups. Rome's birthday is Wednesday 21 April 2027. In 2026 all its events were **free**, with gladiators and re-enactments ([Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/events/birthday-rome-26th-edition-historical-re-enactment-gruppo-storico-romano)). The big parade is usually the Sunday before, so you would likely miss it. Food to try (unverified): supplì, pizza al taglio at Pizzarium or Bonci, carbonara at Roscioli, gelato at Giolitti or Fatamorgana, and a maritozzo cream bun.

**Bologna, Italy and Motor Valley (2-3 nights).** Bologna, Italy is a cheap, fun food city and your base for cars. Ostello degli Artisti has dorms **from €22 (NZ$43)** with breakfast ([ItalyPlanner hostels](https://italyplanner.com/best-hostels-italy.html)). The Ferrari Maranello plus Enzo Ferrari combo costs **from about US$60.50**. A Lamborghini, Ferrari and F1 simulator bundle costs from about US$163 ([Tiqets](https://www.tiqets.com/en/museo-ferrari-maranello-entry-ticket-shuttle-modena-station-ferrari-b18329/)). The public shuttle from Modena, Italy is the weak point. It listed only two departures, and one reviewer waited over an hour. A day tour from Bologna, Italy to Ferrari, Lamborghini and Pagani costs more but saves a tiring day ([Expedia](https://www.expedia.com.my/ms/things-to-do/ferrari-and-lamborghini-museums-pagani-factory-museum-tour-from-bologna.a3095304.activity-details)). Food to try (unverified): tagliatelle al ragù, tortellini, mortadella, and aperitivo near Via del Pratello.

**Gardaland, Italy (1 day, plus a spa day).** Gardaland opened its 2026 season on **28 March**. 2027 is not announced yet, but late April is safe ([EAP Magazin](https://www.eap-magazin.de/Article/Gardaland-Reports-Positive-Results-for-%E2%80%9CMagic-Winter%E2%80%9D.html)). It opens **10:30 to 18:00**. Take the train to Peschiera del Garda, Italy, then the **free shuttle** that runs about every 30 minutes ([The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/milan/gardaland-park/)). Ride Raptor, Oblivion and Blue Tornado first. Go on a weekday, not Sunday 25 April. Sirmione, Italy is next door. Its Aquaria thermal spa costs about €40-60 (NZ$78-117) (unverified). That makes an easy rest day.

**Florence, Italy (2 nights, or a day trip).** The Uffizi costs **€25 plus a €4 fee** in April. Book at least 48 hours ahead ([ItalyPlanner](https://italyplanner.com/cost-of-museum-tickets-italy-2026.html)). The Accademia (David) is €16 plus €4. Florence, Italy is about 40 minutes from Bologna, Italy by fast train (unverified). Food to try (unverified): All'Antico Vinaio sandwiches and gelato at Vivoli.

**Venice, Italy (on the Contiki).** The tour covers the basics. If you add a night, Generator Venice has dorms of **€30-50 (NZ$59-98)** ([ItalyPlanner hostels](https://italyplanner.com/best-hostels-italy.html)).

**Trains.** Fast trains link all these cities. Saver fares run **€19.90-29.90 (NZ$39-58)**. Booking opens about 4 months ahead, so April seats appear around December 2026 to January 2027 ([Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Italy-trains.htm)).

**Holidays to dodge.** Sunday 25 April 2027 is Liberation Day. It is also the last Sunday of the month, when the Vatican can be free. Expect big crowds. Do not plan Gardaland or a big travel day then.

## Germany and Austria: the best coasters and spas sit here

**Europa-Park, Rust, Germany.** The 2027 season start is listed as **7 March 2027** ([europapark.de](https://www.europapark.de/en/overnight/offers-events/pre-opening-weeks-2027)). So April is safe. Online day tickets cost **€67-76 (NZ$131-148)**. Rulantica, the indoor water park next door, costs €41-54 (NZ$80-105) ([MonsterDealz](https://www.monsterdealz.de/magazin/europa-park-tickets)). It makes a good rest day after the park. Freiburg, Germany is a cheaper base (unverified).

**Therme Erding, near Munich, Germany.** This is one of the world's largest spa complexes. A weekday day pass costs **€52 (NZ$101) for pools and slides, or €62 (NZ$121) with the sauna area**. The slides open at **13:00 on weekdays**, which suits a late riser. The sauna area is **adults only and fully nude** ([MonsterDealz](https://www.monsterdealz.de/magazin/therme-erding-preise)). That is normal in Germany and Austria.

**Munich, Germany spring beer festival.** Two big tents serve Augustiner and Spaten. Tent entry is free, with live bands each day ([muenchen.de](https://www.muenchen.de/veranstaltungen/feste-festivals/fruehlingsfest-2026-theresienwiese)). A litre of beer costs about €12-14 (NZ$23-27) (unverified). Wombat's has a Munich, Germany hostel ([Hostelworld](https://www.hostelworld.com/hostels/p/309348/wombat-s-city-hostel-munich-werksviertel/)).

**Stuttgart, Germany: cars plus a second beer festival.** The Porsche Museum costs **€15 (NZ$29), or €12 (NZ$23) from 16:00**. It is closed on Mondays ([porsche.com](https://www.porsche.com/international/aboutporsche/porschemuseum/openinghoursandprices/)). The Mercedes-Benz Museum is next to it in the same city (price unverified). With the spring festival, this is a strong 2-night stop.

**Phantasialand, Brühl near Cologne, Germany.** World-class coasters such as Taron and Black Mamba. Tickets cost **€54-68 (NZ$105-133)** ([Freizeitpark-Welt](https://www.freizeitpark-welt.de/freizeitparks/phantasialand/phantasialand_start.php?id=5)). Its 2027 opening is not confirmed.

**Berlin, Germany.** Best nightlife on the list. Berghain entry is about **€20-30 cash**. Go alone or in a small group, wear dark clothes and stay calm. Sunday is often easier ([EuropeNightlife](https://europenightlife.com/germany/berlin/how-to-get-into-berghain)). Wombat's and Industriepalast are social hostels from about €18-22 ([berlintourism.org](https://www.berlintourism.org/best-hostels-berlin/)). It is far from your routes, so it is a "maybe".

**Vienna, Austria.** A sweet-tooth city. Sachertorte costs about **€8.90 (NZ$17) at Café Sacher** and €8.50 at Demel. Aida is the cheap pick at about €4.50 ([The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/vienna/best-cakes-in-vienna/)). Munich, Germany to Vienna, Austria trains start **from €18** ([rail.cc](https://rail.cc/train/munich-to-vienna)). Great, but it does not fit an 8-day week with your other goals.

## The Netherlands and Belgium: King's Day plus chocolate

**King's Day, Amsterdam, Netherlands.** King's Night is Monday 26 April. King's Day is Tuesday 27 April. Go to the Jordaan for canal parties, Rembrandtplein and Leidseplein for stages and bars, and Museumplein for big concerts. Bring cash, a rain jacket and layers. Watch for pickpockets. Beds **fill months ahead** at much higher prices ([LuggageHero](https://luggagehero.com/blog/kings-day-amsterdam-the-ultimate-guide-to-amsterdams-biggest-street-party/)). A party hostel gives you a ready-made group. St Christopher's at The Winston runs a nightly pub crawl. Flying Pig and Bulldog are other social picks ([Savvy Backpacker](https://thesavvybackpacker.com/city-guide/best-party-hostels-amsterdam/)). Dorms often cost 2-3 times the normal rate that week (unverified). Staying in Haarlem, Netherlands or Utrecht, Netherlands can save money (unverified).

**Amsterdam, Netherlands museums and spas.** The Rijksmuseum costs **€25 (NZ$49)** with timed tickets. The Van Gogh Museum is about the same and **sells out 7-10 days ahead** ([ArtVisitGuide](https://www.artvisitguide.com/rijksmuseum-tickets-guide/)). For a rest day, Spa Zuiver has 8 saunas for about **€30 (NZ$59)**. Saunakaravaan runs on Sundays, 17:00 to 22:00, with live music ([Bonjour Amsterdam](https://bonjouramsterdam.fr/en/sauna-amsterdam/)).

**Louwman Museum, The Hague, Netherlands.** About **250 classic cars** in the world's oldest private car collection. Entry is **€20 (NZ$39)** ([WhichMuseum](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/louwman-museum-the-hague-130/tickets-prices)). It is about 1 hour from Amsterdam, Netherlands by train and closed on Mondays (both unverified).

**Keukenhof, near Lisse, Netherlands.** The 2027 season is reported as **18 March to 9 May**. Entry was about €23 (NZ$45) in 2026 ([Amsterdam Experiences](https://amsterdamexperiences.nl/blog/the-highlights-of-keukenhof-tulip-gardens-in-2027/)). It is flat and easy on your back. Nice, but optional.

**Efteling and Walibi, Netherlands.** Efteling, near Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands, has Baron 1898, a dive coaster with a 123 ft drop ([Theme Park Insider](https://themeparkinsider.com/reviews/efteling)). Walibi Holland, Netherlands has Goliath and Untamed, from €44.50 online ([Freizeitpark-Welt](https://www.freizeitpark-welt.de/freizeitparks/walibi_holland/preise/)). Late April overlaps Dutch school holidays, so expect busier parks (unverified).

**Belgium for sweets.** Brussels, Belgium has Maison Dandoy for waffles and speculoos, plus Mary, Wittamer and Neuhaus for chocolate ([Tootbus](https://www.tootbus.com/en/brussels/inspiration/mouthwatering-guide-best-chocolate-shops-and-bakeries-brussels)). Bruges, Belgium has The Chocolate Line. Antwerp, Belgium has Chocolate Nation, billed as the world's largest Belgian chocolate museum. Ghent, Belgium has De Frietketel for fries ([Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/what-to-eat-and-drink-in-belgium)). For beer, Delirium Café in Brussels, Belgium has over 2,000 beers (unverified). In Bruges, Belgium, Hostel Lybeer runs beer tastings and pub quizzes. Dorms there cost about **€15-23 (NZ$29-45)** ([Hostelgeeks](https://hostelgeeks.com/best-hostels-in-bruges-belgium/)). Trains run from Brussels, Belgium to Bruges, Belgium in about an hour, via Ghent, Belgium ([AmsterdamTips](https://www.amsterdamtips.com/travel-amsterdam-bruges)).

## France and Switzerland: mostly covered by the tour

**Parc Astérix, near Paris, France.** Better for coasters than Disneyland Paris. Toutatis reaches **107 km/h, the fastest in France**. Tickets cost **€56 (NZ$109)** booked 7 days ahead. In 2026 it opened on 4 April, but only on select days ([The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/paris/parc-asterix/)). Check the 2027 calendar.

**Cité de l'Automobile, Mulhouse, France.** The world's biggest car museum. It has **500+ cars and about 150 Bugattis** for €19 (NZ$37) ([JDS](https://www.jds.fr/mulhouse/musee/cite-de-l-automobile-musee-de-l-auto-37_L)). It sits on the Paris, France to Basel, Switzerland train line. That trip takes about 3 hours, from €29 ([Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/Switzerland)). Europa-Park, Germany is close by (about 1 hour, unverified). This pairing is a strong car-and-coaster detour.

**Paris, France hostels.** St Christopher's Gare du Nord has two bars and social nights. Generator Paris has a rooftop bar and dorms from about US$50 ([HostelGeeks](https://hostelgeeks.com/best-party-hostels-in-paris-france/)).

**Switzerland.** Lindt Home of Chocolate costs **CHF 17 (NZ$36)** and is open **10:00-19:00 daily**. Book a time slot ([Zürich Tourism](https://www.zuerich.com/en/visit/culture/lindt-home-of-chocolate)). Jungfraujoch is the famous no-hike mountain trip. But it costs **CHF 224 (NZ$471) return** from Interlaken, Switzerland, or about half with a Half Fare Card ([MySwissAlps](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/jungfraujoch/)). Balmers in Interlaken, Switzerland is rated **9.6 from over 8,000 reviews** ([Hostelworld](https://www.hostelworld.com/hosteldetails.php/Balmers-Hostel/Interlaken/715)).

## Conclusion

The real choice is not which country. It is whether King's Day is worth giving up an Italian week. The calendar makes King's Day fixed and Italy movable.
